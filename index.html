Our little kitchen story

Berries & Biscuits started with a simple idea: the best mornings begin with something homemade. We cook our jams in small batches using fruit picked at its peak, so every jar tastes like the season it came from.

Our menu changes with the calendar, so there's always something new to try. And of course, every jar deserves a fluffy, buttery biscuit to go with it.
